The IronMind Certification Standard

Certification is not awarded for completing a course.

It is earned by demonstrating that you can understand the methodology, live the principles, coach another person responsibly, exercise sound judgment, and represent the IronMind standard with character.

You will be asked to tell your story.

You will be asked to demonstrate your coaching.

You will be asked to explain what you believe.

You will be asked to show where you intend to carry this work.

And ultimately, the question we will ask is simple:

Would we trust you to carry the IronMind name into somebody else's life?

Because when the answer to that question is yes, you haven't simply completed a certification.

You've earned the responsibility to carry it forward.
