Certified Performance Coaching Program

Certification Requirements

Completion of the training program alone does not guarantee certification.

1. Complete the training

Complete the full certification curriculum and demonstrate active engagement and application.

  • Required training sessions
  • Weekly Learn It / Live It / Coach It exercises
  • Candidate workbook assignments
  • Personal reflection exercises
  • Practice coaching labs
  • Fieldwork assignments
  • Required IronMind tools and assessments
  • Coach Standard self-assessments
  • Feedback and developmental activities

2. Tell a personal resilience or leadership story

Prepare a separate 8–10 minute story about a meaningful experience. Choose an experience you’re comfortable sharing. Reviewers assess reflection and how you apply the lesson in service of others.

  1. 1. The Moment What happened?
  2. 2. The Impact Why did the experience matter?
  3. 3. The Internal Struggle What was happening beneath the surface?
  4. 4. The Response What choices, actions, or changes followed?
  5. 5. The Lesson What did the candidate learn about identity, resilience, confidence, leadership, failure, or pressure?
  6. 6. The Coaching Connection How does that lesson affect the way the candidate will coach others?
  7. 7. The Responsibility What does the candidate now feel responsible for carrying forward?

3. Present to IronMind leadership

Plan for 20 minutes of presentation and 10 minutes of questions.

Who you are as a coach

  • Why I chose this work
  • What brought me to IronMind
  • My personal coaching philosophy
  • The experiences that shaped me
  • The population or community I feel called to serve

What you believe

  • Identity before performance
  • Resilience
  • Confidence
  • Grit and standards
  • Pressure
  • Leadership
  • The role of the coach

How you coach

  • How they create trust
  • How they listen
  • How they challenge
  • How they create accountability
  • How they use personal stories responsibly
  • How they recognize coaching boundaries
  • How they help clients maintain ownership

Where will you carry this?

Explain where you plan to use the IronMind methodology and whom you intend to serve.

4. Demonstrate your coaching live

Lead a 30-minute coaching session observed by an approved evaluator, followed by a 10-minute debrief. Be ready to explain why you chose your approach.

  • Presence
  • Listening
  • Question quality
  • Curiosity
  • Identity-first perspective
  • Appropriate challenge
  • Client ownership
  • Use of IronMind frameworks
  • Ability to avoid over-coaching
  • Accountability
  • Boundary recognition
  • Closing with clarity

5. Submit your coaching portfolio

Submit work that shows your development from Week 1 through Week 12.

  • Coach Origin Story
  • Personal Coaching Philosophy
  • IronMind Coach Standard self-assessment
  • Identity exercises
  • Confidence work
  • Resilience reflection
  • Pressure-response work
  • Leadership reflection
  • Transition exercise
  • Fieldwork reflections
  • Coaching feedback
  • Personal Resilience/Leadership Story
  • 90-Day Coach Development Plan

6. Document field coaching

The proposed minimum is five documented sessions outside scheduled training. Omit identifying client details.

  • General topic
  • Framework or skill practiced
  • What the coach noticed
  • What worked
  • What was difficult
  • What the coach would do differently
  • What the coach learned about themselves

7. Sign the Certified Coach Commitment

Character
I put the development of the person ahead of my ego.
Credibility
I prepare, continue learning, and represent my experience honestly.
Competence
I will not claim expertise I do not possess.
Connection
I see the person beneath the performance.
Consistency
I model the preparation, accountability, ownership, and reflection I ask of others.
Responsibility
I treat the influence entrusted to me as stewardship.

8. Receive your leadership decision

Leadership reviews all your evidence and professional readiness.

Certified
You demonstrated readiness to represent the methodology.
Certification Pending
You completed training and will receive a focused development plan and an opportunity for reassessment.
Not Yet Ready
You need more substantial development before representing IronMind as a certified coach.
